LFDT Study Group (LFDTsg) is a community dedicated to learning and mastering Hyperledger Fabric and Zero Knowledge cryptography. 

This platform provides curated resources, tutorials, and documentation for developers, researchers, and enthusiasts who want to dive deep into blockchain technology and privacy-preserving computation.

## What We Offer

- Hyperledger Fabric -  Enterprise blockchain platform resources
- Fabric-X - Next-generation fabric technologies  
- Zero Knowledge - Privacy and cryptography resources

## Getting Started

The journey into blockchain development requires dedication and the right resources. Our study group is designed to guide you through:

1. Understanding core blockchain concepts
2. Hands-on tutorials and exercises
3. Community discussions and collaboration
4. Advanced topics and research
